Wildlife and Conservation

Safari & Wildlife Conservation

Where the Wild Calls

Minutes from Kinyuki Country Hotel lies Ol Pejeta Conservancy, one of Africa's most extraordinary conservation areas and the last sanctuary of the world's Northern White Rhinos. This is a bucket-list wildlife experience matched by very few places on Earth.

Also within easy reach is the renowned Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary, home to rescued chimpanzees in a natural setting, a deeply moving and educational experience for all ages.

  • Northern White Rhino encounter at Ol Pejeta
  • Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary visit
  • Game drives across the Laikipia savannah
  • Expert naturalist guides available
  • Sunrise and sunset game drive options

Alpine Adventure and Nature

Alpine Adventure & Hiking

Stand Above the World

Experience the raw, breathtaking grandeur of Mount Kenya, Africa's second-highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Kinyuki Country Hotel is the ideal base for guided ascents via the Sirimon Gate, celebrated as the mountain's most scenic and rewarding route.

For a different kind of wonder, the enchanting Ngare Ndare Forest offers thrilling canopy walks suspended high above shimmering azure rock pools, an experience unlike anything else in Kenya.

  • Guided Mount Kenya hikes via Sirimon Gate
  • Canopy walk at Ngare Ndare Forest
  • Azure rock pool swimming
  • Forest birding and nature walks
  • All experience levels catered for

Equestrian and Savannah Biking

Equestrian Culture & Savannah Biking

Roam Free Across Laikipia

Experience the Laikipia savannah at its most intimate, on horseback or by mountain bike. Our bespoke equestrian arrangements allow guests to connect with the landscape in a way that motorised safaris cannot replicate. Feel the warm equatorial breeze, hear the sounds of wildlife, and move through the wilderness on your own terms.

Mountain biking trails traverse some of the most scenic terrain in the entire Mount Kenya region, suitable for enthusiastic beginners and experienced riders alike.

  • Bespoke horseback safari arrangements
  • Guided mountain biking trails
  • Sunrise horseback rides
  • Scenic savannah landscapes
  • Fully supported group rides
