The HoneyComb Restaurant

The Epicentre of Local & Global Flavours

A sophisticated, high-ceilinged dining hall merging authentic Kenyan heritage with global fine dining, featuring an open-plan interactive kitchen using organic produce harvested at the foot of Mount Kenya. Our celebrated Sunday Country Brunch features live meat carving stations and a world-class dessert buffet, an institution in Nanyuki.

The Stinger Lounge

Where Luxury Meets Rhythm

Nanyuki's premier high-energy lounge crafted with contemporary industrial-chic aesthetics and plush seating. Our resident mixologists pour regional signature cocktails from a curated menu, and our temperature-controlled wine cellar showcases an exceptional selection. Equipped with a state-of-the-art acoustic sound system hosting international DJs, live jazz bands, and exclusive themed brand activations.

Stinger Pool Bar & Grill

Sun-Drenched Relaxation

A tropical-inspired outdoor oasis overlooking our heated infinity pool and manicured grounds, the ultimate Nanyuki setting for Sundowners. Our wood-fired pizzas, gourmet sliders, and chilled tropical smoothies are legendary. Every weekend features the renowned Executive Mbuzi Roast on the open-air grill, a Kinyuki tradition not to be missed.

The Kinyuki Cafe & Bakery

Artisanal Comfort in Every Sip

A cosy, aromatic boutique cafe tailored for remote executives, writers, and discerning travellers. Single-origin Kenyan coffees sourced from the slopes of Mount Kenya, handcrafted sourdough breads, fresh artisan pastries, and gourmet deli sandwiches. Start your day with the Explorer's Breakfast before an early safari, or conduct casual meetings over an exquisite afternoon high tea.
