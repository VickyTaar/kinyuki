The Kinyuki Story

The Kinyuki
Experience

Kinyuki Country Hotel is a contemporary luxury country hotel located in Nanyuki, Laikipia County, Kenya. We offer a seamless blend of comfort, leisure, dining, and events. Designed as a versatile hospitality destination, the hotel caters to leisure travellers, corporate clients, event organisers, and international tourists seeking quality service and memorable experiences.

With a total of 124 rooms, expansive grounds, modern facilities, and diverse food and beverage outlets, Kinyuki Country Hotel positions itself as a one-stop destination for accommodation, dining, entertainment, conferencing, and social events within the greater Nanyuki region.

Our Vision

To be the leading country hotel in Nanyuki, recognised for exceptional service, vibrant experiences, and memorable guest journeys.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality hospitality experiences through personalised service, comfortable accommodation, diverse dining, and well-curated events.
