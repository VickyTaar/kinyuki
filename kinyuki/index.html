The Kinyuki Experience

A Contemporary Country House

Kinyuki Hotel is a contemporary luxury country hotel in Nanyuki, Laikipia County, Kenya. A seamless blend of comfort, leisure, dining, and events — designed as a versatile hospitality destination for leisure travellers, corporate clients, event organisers, and international guests seeking quality service and memorable experiences.

With 124 rooms, expansive grounds, modern facilities, and diverse dining outlets, Kinyuki positions itself as a one-stop destination for accommodation, dining, entertainment, conferencing, and social events in the greater Nanyuki region.

Vision

To be the leading country hotel in Nanyuki, recognised for exceptional service, vibrant experiences, and memorable guest journeys.

Mission

To deliver high-quality hospitality through personalised service, comfortable accommodation, diverse dining, and well-curated events that exceed expectations.

4 Distinct Dining Concepts

Gastronomy & Social Spaces

Dining at Kinyuki is an immersive experience. From farm-to-table breakfast spreads to evening cocktails, our four distinct outlets deliver world-class culinary artistry in the heart of Laikipia.

HoneyComb Restaurant — high-ceilinged dining hall with hardwood tables
Outlet 01

The HoneyComb Restaurant

"The Epicentre of Local & Global Flavours"

A sophisticated, high-ceilinged dining hall merging authentic Kenyan heritage with global fine dining. Our open-plan interactive kitchen uses organic produce harvested at the foot of Mount Kenya.

Famous for its regional Sunday Country Brunch featuring live meat carving stations and a world-class dessert buffet.

300+ Capacity  ·  Indoor & Al Fresco  ·  Sunday Brunch

Stinger Lounge — contemporary booths with accent lighting
Outlet 02

The Stinger Lounge

"Where Luxury Meets Rhythm"

Nanyuki's premier high-energy lounge crafted with contemporary industrial-chic aesthetics and plush seating. Resident mixologists pour regional signature cocktails from our curated wine cellar.

State-of-the-art acoustic sound system hosting international DJs, live jazz bands, and exclusive themed brand activations.

500+ Capacity  ·  Live DJ & Jazz  ·  Curated Wine Cellar

The Stinger Pool Bar & Grill

"Sun-Drenched Relaxation"

A tropical-inspired outdoor oasis overlooking the heated pool and manicured grounds. The setting for Nanyuki's finest sundowners — wood-fired pizzas, gourmet sliders, and chilled tropical smoothies.
