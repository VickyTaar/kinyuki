How to Find Us

Getting Here

By Air, Nanyuki Airstrip

Charter flights from Wilson Airport, Nairobi take approximately 45 minutes. We offer complimentary airport transfer for all guests.

By Road from Nairobi

A scenic 3-hour drive from Nairobi via the Thika Superhighway and Nanyuki Road (B5). Self-drive and chauffeur options are available.

By Public Transport

Regular bus and matatu services operate between Nairobi and Nanyuki Town, approximately 3 to 4 hours travel time.

Strategic Position

Our Geographic Advantage

On the Equator

Experience the rare distinction of staying on the Equator line, simultaneously in the northern and southern hemispheres.

Aviation Access

Just 5 minutes from Nanyuki Airstrip, providing fast chartered access for high-profile guests and corporate delegations from Nairobi.

Wildlife Gateway

Minutes from Ol Pejeta Conservancy, Mount Kenya National Park, and the Samburu game reserves, world-class safari at your doorstep.
